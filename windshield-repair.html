Why Repair Your Windshield Instead of Replacing It?

When a rock hits your windshield, it creates a small chip. Left untreated, temperature changes and road vibrations cause that chip to crack — spreading across your entire windshield. What started as a $75 repair can become a $500 replacement.

Our repair process:

We inject a special resin into the chip or crack that bonds with your existing glass, restoring structural integrity and optical clarity. The result is barely visible — and your windshield is saved.

When Can Windshield Repair Be Done?

Our technicians can repair your windshield if:

  • The chip or crack is smaller than 6 inches
  • The damage is not in the driver's direct line of sight
  • There are no more than 3 damage points on the glass
  • The damage is not too close to the edge of the windshield

Does Insurance Cover Windshield Repair?

In Pennsylvania, comprehensive auto insurance typically covers windshield repair with no deductible. That's right — you pay nothing out of pocket for repairs. We'll handle all the insurance paperwork for you.
