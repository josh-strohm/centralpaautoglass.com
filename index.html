Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about auto glass repair in Altoona or State College? We've got answers.

How much does auto glass repair cost in Altoona, PA? +

Windshield repair starts at $75, and full replacement starts from $250 depending on your vehicle. Most insurance claims have $0 deductible for glass repair, meaning you pay nothing out of pocket. We work with all insurance providers in Pennsylvania and handle all the paperwork for you.

How long does windshield replacement take? +

Standard windshield replacement takes 60-90 minutes. After installation, we recommend waiting 1 hour before driving. We offer same-day service and mobile appointments throughout Altoona, State College, and all surrounding areas in Centre and Blair County.

Do you offer mobile auto glass service in Centre County? +

Yes! Our mobile service covers all of Centre County and Blair County. We'll come to your home, workplace, or any location in State College, Altoona, Bellefonte, Hollidaysburg, or anywhere in between. Mobile service is available Monday through Saturday.

Will my insurance cover windshield replacement in Pennsylvania? +

In Pennsylvania, comprehensive insurance typically covers auto glass repair with no deductible. Pennsylvania law requires insurance companies to waive the deductible for windshield repair. We'll handle all insurance paperwork and bill directly, so you pay nothing out of pocket in most cases.

Can you fix a cracked windshield? +

Yes, if the crack is smaller than 6 inches and not in the driver's direct line of sight, we can repair it in under 30 minutes at a fraction of replacement cost. Repairing early prevents the crack from spreading and restores the structural integrity of your windshield.

Do you offer same-day auto glass service? +

Yes! We offer same-day appointments throughout Centre and Blair County. Call before noon and we'll try to get to you the same day. Emergency after-hours service is available for urgent situations. Just call our emergency line.
