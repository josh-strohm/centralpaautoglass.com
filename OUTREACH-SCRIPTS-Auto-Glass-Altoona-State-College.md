# Auto Glass Shop Outreach Scripts
## Website Landlord Business - Altoona & State College, PA

---

## OVERVIEW

The key to closing auto glass shops is **showing them value first, asking for nothing in return, and letting the results speak for themselves**.

The strategy:
1. Build the website and get it ranking FIRST
2. Let the site collect leads (calls) for 2-4 weeks
3. Approach shops with PROOF (actual call recordings, lead data)
4. Offer a free trial week
5. Convert to monthly flat fee

---

## SCRIPT 1: Initial Cold Call (First Contact)

**When to call:** After site has been live 2-4 weeks and collecting calls

**Goal:** Get them to accept a free trial, NOT close the sale

**Phone Script:**

---

> **You:** Hey, what's up man? This is [Your Name].
>
> **Them:** Uh, hi? Who's this?
>
> **You:** Yeah, so real quick - I got a website that's been getting a ton of calls for auto glass repair in [City]. And I'm not an auto glass guy, I don't have a shop or anything. I'm just trying to figure out what to do with all these calls.
>
> **Them:** Wait, what kind of website?
>
> **You:** Yeah, so I built this site about auto glass repair, right? Started ranking on Google for people searching for that in [Altoona/State College area]. And I've been getting like 30-50 calls a month from people literally typing in "auto glass repair near me" and finding my site.
>
> **Them:** Okay... so what do you want?
>
> **You:** Nothing, man. I'm just trying to be helpful. I've got all these people calling me looking for auto glass services and I can't help them because I'm not in the business. So I'm wondering if you'd want me to send them your way? You interested?
>
> **Them:** I mean... yeah, I guess. How would that work?
>
> **You:** Nah, it's simple. You give me your email and phone number, and whenever someone calls my site looking for [City] auto glass, I'll just forward them straight to you. No cost, no obligation. Just a free week to see if the leads are actually worth anything to you.
>
> **Them:** That's... weird. Why are you doing this?
>
> **You:** [Chuckle] I know, right? It sounds too good to be true. But here's the thing - I already built the website. I already did the SEO. The website's already getting calls. I just need someone to actually answer the phone when those calls come in. And you're a local shop, so it makes sense.
>
> **Them:** So what do I have to do?
>
> **You:** Literally nothing except pick up the phone when it rings. And if you get some jobs out of it and want to keep getting those leads, we can talk about some kind of monthly arrangement. But for the first week? Free. No strings.
>
> **Them:** Hmm... I don't know. What kind of calls are you getting?
>
> **You:** Real ones, man. People looking to get their windshields fixed or replaced. Insurance claims, cash jobs, all of it. They're not window shoppers - they're ready to hire someone right now. That's why they're calling multiple shops.
>
> **Them:** Okay... what do you need from me?
>
> **You:** Just your phone number and email so I can set up the forwarding. And I'll send you a quick text confirming everything so you know it's legit. Sound good?
>
> **Them:** Yeah, okay. [Gives contact info]
>
> **You:** Perfect. I'm gonna send you a test call in the next few minutes so you can see what the setup looks like. After that, you're gonna start getting real calls from actual customers. I'll check in with you in a week and we can talk about next steps if you want. No pressure. Sound good?
>
> **Them:** Yeah, alright. Thanks.
>
> **You:** Cool. Talk soon, man.

## SCRIPT 2: Follow-Up Call (After Free Trial)

**When to call:** 5-7 days after starting the free trial

**Goal:** Convert them to a monthly paying client

---

> **You:** Hey [Name], it's [Your Name] again. How's it going?
>
> **Them:** Hey! Yeah, I've been getting some calls. They're pretty good actually.
>
> **You:** Yeah? That's awesome. So here's the thing - I want to keep these leads coming to you because they're clearly valuable. How many jobs do you think you've gotten from the calls I've sent you?
>
> **Them:** I've gotten... actually I've gotten three estimates out of it so far. One's looking pretty solid.
>
> **You:** That's awesome. And what's the average job size for you on an auto glass job? Like when you replace a windshield, what are you charging?
>
> **Them:** Usually around [$400-600] for full replacements.
>
> **You:** Okay, so if you're converting at even one job a week from my leads, that's what - [$400-800] in revenue? Maybe more?
>
> **Them:** Yeah, something like that.
>
> **You:** Cool. So here's what I'm thinking - I can keep sending you these leads on a ongoing basis. But I need to cover my costs for running the website and stuff. So what I'd like to do is just a simple flat monthly fee. [Pause] I'm thinking [Amount - e.g., $500-600] per month.
>
> **Them:** That's... I don't know. That's pretty high.
>
> **You:** Look, I get it. But think about it this way - if you're getting even just one job a month from my leads, that's paying for itself and then some. And I've already proven to you that the leads are real. You've seen the calls come in. You've talked to the customers. You know they're quality.
>
> **Them:** But $500 is a lot.
>
> **You:** [Name], here's my question for you - if I turned off these leads tomorrow, what would happen to your business?
>
> **Them:** I mean... I'd still have my other leads from other places.
>
> **You:** Yeah, but you'd be losing [X] calls a month. And every one of those calls is a potential $400-600 job. You really want to give that up for [$500] a month? That's like one windshield job.
>
> **Them:** [Silence]
>
> **You:** Plus, there's no contract. You want to cancel, you cancel. I'm not locking you into anything. I just want to keep the leads flowing your way because you're good at what you do.
>
> **Them:** I don't know... let me think about it.
>
> **You:** Okay. But here's the thing - if you cancel, those calls stop going to you. And I've got other shops in the area that have been asking me about the leads. So I can't hold them for you forever.
>
> **Them:** Other shops? Like who?
>
> **You:** Yeah, I've been getting calls from [Nearby City] too, so I've had a couple shops ask me about that area. But I like working with you, so I wanted to give you first shot at keeping this going.
>
> **Them:** Hmm. Okay. How much did you say again?
>
> **You:** [$500] a month. Flat fee. No per-lead charges, no surprises. You get unlimited calls from our site for that price.
>
> **Them:** Can you do lower?
>
> **You:** [Pause] You know what? [$450]. But that's my final offer. And honestly? The reason I'm not negotiating is because I know how much these leads are worth to you. I've already seen it. The data doesn't lie.
>
> **Them:** Okay... let me try it for a month and see how it goes.
>
> **You:** Perfect. I'll send you an invoice today. And I'll set up the recurring so you don't have to worry about it. You ready to get back to focusing on your shop? I'll take care of the rest.
>
> **Them:** Alright, sounds good. Thanks man.
>
> **You:** Yeah. Talk soon.

## SCRIPT 6: Handle Objections - "It's Too Expensive"

**Objection:** "I can't afford $500/month"

**Response:**
> I totally understand. But here's what I'd ask you to consider - what's one windshield replacement worth to you? $400? $500? $600? If you're getting even one job a month from my leads, you're breaking even. And if you're getting two, you're way ahead. The math just makes sense.

**If they push back more:**
> Look, I can help you track exactly how many jobs you're getting from my leads. If after a month you're not making back at least what you're paying me, I'll refund your first month. How's that?

## SCRIPT 7: Handle Objections - "I Need To Think About It"

**Response:**
> Yeah, totally. Take a few days to think it over. But just so you know, I'm getting other shops asking about these leads, so I can't hold them forever. If I don't hear back from you by [Day], I'm going to assume you're not interested and move on to the next shop.

**Key point:** Create urgency without being aggressive. They need to know this opportunity has a shelf life.

I'm just offering to send you qualified leads that you don't have to do anything to get. Think of it like... you're getting free advertising. If it doesn't work out, you cancel. No harm done.

## HOW TO FIND SHOPS TO CALL

**Search on Google Maps:**
1. Go to Google Maps
2. Search "auto glass repair" in [Altoona/State College]
3. Copy the business name, phone, and address into a spreadsheet
4. Sort by rating (lower ratings = easier to get, more desperate)
5. Start calling from the bottom of the list first

**Search terms to use:**
- "auto glass repair near me"
- "windshield repair near me"
- "auto glass shop"
- "windshield replacement"

**Look for:**
- Shops with 3.5 stars or lower (more desperate for leads)
- Shops with few reviews
- Shops that have been in business a while but have weak web presence

## THE KEY PRINCIPLES

### 1. Lead with value
Never start by asking for something. Start by offering something for free. The free trial is your foot in the door.

### 2. Be the solution to their problem
They're not thinking "I need a lead gen service." They're thinking "I wish I had more customers." Position yourself as someone helping them solve THAT problem.

### 3. Use social proof
"Other shops have been asking me about these leads" is a powerful line. It creates urgency without lying.

### 4. Align incentives
Their success = your success. If they're making money from your leads, they'll never want to cancel.

### 5. Don't be desperate
You have the leverage. You have the website. You have the leads. They're the ones who need you more than you need them.

### 6. Keep it simple
No contracts. No complicated pricing. Just $X per month, cancel anytime. That's it.

---

## WHAT TO SAY WHEN THEY ASK "HOW MUCH?"

Never give a price before they see the value. Here's the flow:

1. **First call:** Don't mention price. Just offer free trial.
2. **Second call:** Only discuss price after they've seen the leads.
3. **When asked about price:** Give the flat fee, don't negotiate much.
4. **If they push back:** Ask them "how much is one windshield job worth to you?"
